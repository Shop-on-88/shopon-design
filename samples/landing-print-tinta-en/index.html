FAQ

Before you order

What do I need to send for the free layout?

Your logo, business name, contact details and any photos you want to use. If you don't have photos, we can use simple graphic designs instead.

Can I mix designs in one calendar order?

Yes. The free layout covers one design; each extra design is ₱300. Quantities of all designs are added together for the minimum.

How do I pay?

50% down payment once you approve the proof, and the balance on pickup or delivery. Cash, bank transfer and e-wallet transfer are accepted.

Can you print my own finished design file?

Of course. Send a print-ready PDF and we'll check the size and bleed before printing — no layout fee needed.

Is the rush option always available?

Rush depends on the day's print schedule. We'll confirm when we send your quote, before you pay anything.
