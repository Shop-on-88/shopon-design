02 — Choose your burda

Three ways to
embroider light.

Burda means embroidery. Pick a technique, then choose a motif with our embroiderer — sampaguita, calachuchi, rice stalks, or something personal like initials hidden in the vine.

Calado openwork

Threads are drawn out of the fabric and bound into a tiny lattice, so light passes through the pattern itself. Crisp, architectural, very traditional.

Classic panels+ ₱1,500

Sombrado shadow-work

Stitched from the back of a sheer fabric, so the motif glows through as a soft, smoky shadow with a fine outline on top. Quiet and romantic.

Sheer fabrics+ ₱2,200

Hand-painted pintado

Flowers brushed directly onto the fabric with textile paint, then edged in gold thread. Colorful, modern and a favorite for debut escorts and Filipiniana sleeves.

Any fabric+ ₱1,800

Is the fitting consultation really free?

Yes. The consultation, measurements and swatch session cost nothing and there is no obligation to order. If you do order, the 10% made-to-measure discount applies when your order is confirmed by December 15.

How long does a made-to-measure barong take?

Around three weeks from your fitting to pickup for standard embroidery. Fully embroidered piña-seda or larger entourage orders can take longer, so we'll give you a clear date at your consultation.

Do you make Filipiniana and terno too?

We do — Filipiniana blouses, Maria Clara sets and modern terno pieces for mothers, ninangs and debutantes. These are quoted individually at your fitting because sleeves and skirts vary so much.

Can I bring my own fabric or a family barong to copy?

You're welcome to. We can work with fabric you supply (after checking yardage), or take a pattern from a barong you love and adjust it to your measurements.

What do I wear under a sheer barong?

A camisa de chino or a fitted white undershirt. We can make a matching camisa so the line under the sheer looks clean — just mention it on the form.

How do I pay?

A 50% down payment confirms your order and reserves embroidery time; the balance is settled at final fitting. Cash and bank transfer are accepted at the atelier.
