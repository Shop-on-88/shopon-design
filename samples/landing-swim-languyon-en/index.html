The pathway

Four levels, one happy swimmer

The trial lesson tells us where your child starts. Every level ends with a small water-skills check your child can be proud of.

Ages 4–6

Bubbles

Blowing bubbles, face in the water, getting comfy in the shallow end.

Ages 4–8

Floaters

Floating on front and back, gliding to the wall, safe entries.

Ages 6–10

Kickers

Strong flutter kicks with a board, rhythmic breathing, short swims.

Ages 7–12

Strokers

Freestyle and backstroke across the full 25-meter lane.

Questions

Good to know before the first splash

My child is scared of water. Is the trial still okay?

Yes. The Bubbles group starts in the shallow end with games and lots of breaks. Nobody is pushed to go under.

Can I stay and watch?

Of course. The parent deck has seats with a full view of the lanes.

What should we bring?

A swimsuit, towel, slippers and a water bottle. Goggles and swim caps can be borrowed for the trial.

What if we need to move the slot?

Just reply to our confirmation text at least a day before and we'll find another time.
