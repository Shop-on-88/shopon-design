FAQ

Before you book

Do I have to be home for the visit?

Someone needs to be there to open the gate area and talk through the pattern. It can be a family member or caretaker if you send us their number.

Can you work with my existing posts?

Usually, yes. We check them on the visit. If a post is cracked or leaning, we quote a new steel post as a separate line so you can decide.

How long until the gate is installed?

Seven to ten working days after the down payment and your approval of the final sketch. Installation takes most of one day.

Do you remove the old gate?

Yes, on installation day. We can haul it away, or leave it with you if you want to sell it as scrap.

How do I pay?

Fifty percent down payment by bank transfer or cash at the shop, and the balance on installation day once you've checked the gate.
