How a morning runs

Pandesal is at its softest within the hour it leaves the oven, so the whole route is timed around that hour.

4:30

Into the pugon

Dough rolled in breadcrumbs the night before goes into the wood-fired oven, tray by tray.

5:30

Counted and named

Each bag gets your house number written on the fold, so riders never have to knock.

5:45

Riders leave

Three bikes, three loops. Bags ride in covered baskets lined with cloth.

6:30

Last gate

Every bag on the route is hung by 6:30, before the first jeep fills up on the main road.

Before you sign up

What if it rains?

On wet mornings the paper bag goes inside a clear plastic sleeve before it is hung, and riders wear capes over the baskets.

We have no gate, we're in a condo

Bags for Kalachuchi Homes and other buildings are left at the guard desk with your unit number on the fold.

How do I pay?

Every Sunday you get a text with the week's total. Pay by e-wallet or leave cash in an envelope on the gate for Monday's rider.

Can I pause while we travel?

Yes. Text the dates and those mornings are skipped and not billed. There is no minimum after the first month.
