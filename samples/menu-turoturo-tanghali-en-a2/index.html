Talk to us about this design

Every ulam is drawn the way customers see it in the case — from above, in a tray — so they can point at the menu as easily as at the food.

Plate combos get their own dark box with big prices, so the lunch rush can order without adding up trays.

What makes it work

  • Daily ulam sits in one case area, so only that block changes when the menu rotates
  • Small labels (Sabaw, Gulay, Spicy) help regulars spot their usual fast
  • Rice, add-ons and drinks share one tidy two-column box
  • Prints well as a wall menu above the counter or a tabletop card
