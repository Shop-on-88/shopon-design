How a delivery goes

Two weighings, one swap

The tank is weighed on the depot scale when it is filled and again on the bike scale at your door. Both numbers go on the slip you keep.

  1. Text or call your order

    Send your name, street and house number, tank size and the time that suits you. We text back the rider's name and a time window.

  2. Filled and weighed at the depot

    We check the seal cap, weigh the empty tank, fill it, and weigh it again. The rider carries the slip with both numbers.

  3. Weighed again at your door

    The rider sets our platform scale on your floor and puts the tank on it. You read the needle yourself before paying.

  4. Old tank out, new tank in

    We connect the regulator if you want us to, do a soap test on the joints, and take the empty tank back to the depot.

What we look at before we leave

The rider goes over the parts between your tank and your stove and tells you what he sees. It takes about ten minutes.

Hose

We bend it along its length to look for cracks and hard spots, check the date printed on it, and make sure both clamps are tight.

Regulator

We check that it locks onto the valve without wobbling and that the rubber washer inside is still soft and in place.

Seal cap

You see the seal on the new tank before we open it. If it is broken or missing, we take that tank back and bring another.

Soap test

Soapy water goes on the valve, the regulator and both hose ends with the gas on. We watch for bubbles together.

If a part looks worn we show it to you and quote the replacement, hose ₱180, regulator ₱650. You decide whether to change it.

Questions

Before you order

I don't have a tank yet. Can I still order?

Yes. We bring a new cylinder with a one-time deposit, ₱1,150 for 2.7 kg, ₱2,400 for 11 kg. After that you only pay for refills, and the deposit comes back if you return the tank.

Which valve types do you carry?

Snap-on (compact) and screw-type valves for 2.7 kg and 11 kg, screw-type for 22 kg. Tell us which regulator you have, or send a photo, and we bring the matching tank.

What if the weight at my door is short?

We write the door weight on the slip next to the depot weight. If the gas comes up under the size you ordered, we take the difference off the price right there or bring a top-up the same day.

Can my carinderia get a standing weekly drop?

Yes. Pick a day and a tank count. We come at the same time each week and text the evening before so you can skip a week if you still have gas.

Do you connect the tank for me?

If you ask, the rider fits the regulator and does the soap test with you watching. If you prefer to do it yourself, he leaves the tank on the floor with the seal on.
