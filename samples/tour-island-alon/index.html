How a day goes

From pier to
pink sky.

A relaxed pace with plenty of water time. The crew adjusts stops to tide and wind, so the order may change on the day.

7:30

Meet at the pier

Quick safety talk, life vests on, dry bags for your phone.

8:30

First lagoon

Glass-green water and limestone walls. Kayak or swim in.

12:00

Beach lunch

Grilled fish, pork skewers, rice, fruit — set up by the crew on the sand.

1:30

Swim & snorkel

Calm coves and a shallow reef. Gear and guides on hand.

3:30

Back to town

Cold water and towels on the ride home. Hotel drop-off on request.

Good to know

Questions before
you sail.

What if the weather turns bad?

If the coast guard suspends trips, we move you to the next open date or refund your deposit in full.

Can kids and non-swimmers join?

Yes. Life vests fit kids and adults, and the crew stays close during every swim stop.

How do I pay?

A 30% deposit holds your seats. The balance is paid at the pier in cash or by bank transfer.

Can we book a private boat?

Private charters are available for groups. Send a request with your date and group size and we'll quote you.

Still unsure which route fits?

Tell us your dates and group. We'll suggest a route based on tides and your pace.
