Kawa Lomi House

A wall menu for a Batangas-style lomi house in Lipa that stays open until 2 AM for commuters and late barkadas. The top half is one big bowl seen from above, thick miki noodles under a glossy egg-thickened broth, with every topping named on the side so first-timers know what they are ordering. Below it, the bowls and the add-ons sit side by side, then pancit, drinks and a small guide to mixing the soy, calamansi and sili dip.
