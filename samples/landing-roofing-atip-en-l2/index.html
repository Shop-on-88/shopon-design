The five things we check on every roof

The numbers match the drawing above. Each one gets a photo in the report we send to your phone after the visit.

1

Rusted sheets

Brown patches, pinholes and thinning along the overlaps of your GI sheets.

2

Loose screws

Lifted roof screws and cracked rubber washers where rain gets pushed in by wind.

3

Ridge roll

Gaps and missing sealant along the top where both sides of the roof meet.

4

Gutters

Leaves, mud and sagging brackets that make water spill back under the sheets.

5

Ceiling and trusses

Water stains on the ceiling board and soft or wet wood in the roof frame.

Questions before you book

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. There is no visit fee within Quezon City. For areas outside QC we may add a transport fee, and we tell you the amount before we confirm the slot.

Do you work on two-storey houses?

Yes. Two-storey roofs need extra safety rigging, which adds ₱500 per job. The inspection is still free.

Can you replace only a few sheets?

Yes, as long as the frame underneath is sound. If the purlins are rotten we show you the photos and price the frame repair separately.

How do I pay?

Half when materials are delivered and half when the work is done, in cash or bank transfer. You get a receipt for both.
