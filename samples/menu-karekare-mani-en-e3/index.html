Mani Street Kare‑Kare House

A one-page table menu for a family kare-kare house in Quezon City, built around the dish everyone comes for. The upper half is a clay palayok drawn at a three-quarter angle, filled with oxtail rounds, honeycomb tripe, pechay, sitaw and eggplant in a thick peanut sauce, with a saucer of bagoong beside it. Next to it the three pot sizes are priced in large type, then the choice of meat, the three bagoong levels, the ulam list, rice and extras, sweets and drinks, and a three-step note on how to eat it.
