What happens in the chair

The colour match is a separate short visit, so you can think about the price at home before booking the fitting.

Match by the window

We hold ring swatches at the root, mid-length and ends in daylight. Two shades are blended if your ends are lighter.

15 min

Strand test

A small piece is fitted behind your ear to check weight and how the tape or bond sits on your hair.

Aftercare

Keeping wefts smooth at home

A printed care card comes with every fitting. These are the four things we repeat most.

Brush from the ends

Hold the hair above the tapes or bonds and work up from the tips with a loop brush.

Dry the roots first

Point the dryer at the attachments on low heat before the lengths, so tape stays dry.

Sleep in a loose braid

One soft braid keeps the lengths from knotting on the pillow overnight.

Store clip-ins flat

Lay each piece in the satin pouch, clips closed, away from the bathroom steam.

Questions

Before you book

Will extensions damage my own hair?

Weight is the main thing. We match the grams to the thickness of your own hair and space the rows so no single section carries too much. Keep move-up dates and use our remover, never pull tapes off at home.

Can I colour or curl the extension hair?

Curling with heat up to 180°C is fine. Going darker can be done in the studio; going lighter is not recommended, so we match the shade you want from the start.

My hair is short. Is 24 inches possible?

If your hair reaches at least the chin, tape-ins can blend. Under that, keratin-tips or a custom wig usually look more natural. We will tell you honestly at the colour match.

Can I reuse the hair at move-up?

Yes, tape-in wefts are cleaned and re-taped two or three times. Keratin-tips are single use.

How do I pay and is there a deposit?

A 30% deposit holds the hair once you approve the price card. The rest is paid on fitting day in cash, card or bank transfer.
