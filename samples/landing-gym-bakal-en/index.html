Good
questions

Something else on your mind? Text us at 0900 000 0000 or email hello@example.com.

Q1I've never lifted before. Is this for me?

Yes — most people start here as beginners. Day one is an intro session where a coach shows you around, teaches the basics and suggests which classes to try. Every class has lighter options.

Q2What should I bring?

Comfortable workout clothes, closed rubber shoes, a water bottle, a small towel and a valid ID for check-in. Locks for the day-use lockers are at the front desk.

Q3Is the pass really free?

Yes, ₱0. No card details and no deposit. It's one pass per person, for ages 16 and up (16–17 with a parent's consent).

Q4Do the three days have to be in a row?

No. Pick any three days within seven days of your start day — for example Monday, Wednesday and Saturday.

Q5Can I bring a friend?

Please do. Each friend just claims their own pass, and you can book the same classes so you train together.

Q6What happens after the three days?

Nothing automatic. If you'd like to stay, the first-month promo of ₱1,990 is yours when you join within 14 days. If not, no hard feelings — come say hi anytime.
