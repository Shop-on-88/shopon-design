What happens to your pair

The ticket stays on the laces from drop-off to claim, so pairs never get mixed up.

  1. Drop off or pickup

    Bring them to the shop or book a rider. We write a claim number on a paper ticket.

  2. Check and text

    We look at the material, glue and stains, then text you a photo and the price before washing.

  3. Clean and dry

    Hand brushing by material, then air drying on shoe trees for at least 24 hours. No washing machine.

  4. Claim

    Show the ticket number at the counter, or the rider brings them back in a cloth bag.

We brush by material

Each material gets a different brush and cleaner. Tell us what yours are made of, or we'll check at drop-off.

Canvas

Soft nylon brush and mild foam

Mesh

Foam only, dabbed, never scrubbed

Smooth leather

Cleaner, then conditioner to keep it soft

Suede

Brass and crepe brush, no water soak

Knit

Cool water, flat drying to keep the shape

We don't take pairs with cracked soles that are coming apart, or boots taller than the ankle.

Questions

Anything else, message us on Messenger: @example.

Will every stain come out?

Not always. Old ink, dye transfer and deep yellowing in mesh may only fade. We tell you on the photo text before we start, so you can decide.

How do I pay?

Cash or e-wallet when you claim the pair. For pickups, pay the rider on return.

What if I can't claim on time?

We keep pairs for 30 days after the ready date. Just text us your claim number if you need more time.

Do you clean slides and sandals?

Yes, rubber and foam slides are ₱200 a pair. Leather sandals follow the smooth leather price.
