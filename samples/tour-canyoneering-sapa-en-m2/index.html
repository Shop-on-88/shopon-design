Your day

Back at your hotel by mid-afternoon

Times are for the full route. Half canyon starts at the narrows at 09:30 and joins the full group for lunch.

  1. 06:00
    Pickup in Moalboal

    Van leaves from the public beach road. Other pickups on request.

  2. 07:00
    Gear and briefing

    Fit helmets and vests, practise the jump position on dry land, meet your guide.

  3. 07:30
    Into the canyon

    Stops 1 to 3, with time to repeat the slides.

  4. 09:30
    The narrows

    Float and swim through the gorge. Half-canyon guests join here.

  5. 11:30
    The falls

    Raft ride and free time in the basin.

  6. 12:30
    Lunch on the bank

    Grilled fish, rice and fruit. Vegetarian plate if you tell us when booking.

  7. 14:00
    Van back

    Showers and lockers at the base before you leave.

Safety
1:4

One guide for every four guests, never more, on every trip and every date.

  • Helmet and vest stay on

    From the briefing until you reach the lunch bank, including in calm pools.

  • Non-swimmers are welcome

    The vest keeps you floating. A guide stays within arm's reach through the narrows.

  • We cancel when the river is brown

    After heavy rain the water rises and turns cloudy. You get a new date or your money back.

  • Guides check every landing

    Before each jump a guide swims the pool to check depth and clear branches.

Questions

Before you book

Still unsure? Message us with your group's ages and swimming level and we will suggest a trip.

I can't swim. Can I still come?

Yes. Most of the route is floating with the current, and the vest keeps your head above water. Choose the half canyon if you would rather skip the deep entry pools.

Do I have to jump?

No. Every jump above three metres has a path around it. Stop 1 is a 3 m jump or a short scramble down with a guide holding your hand.

What is the youngest age?

Eight for the half canyon and ten for the full route. Children need to wear the junior vest, which we bring for every booking with kids.

Can I bring my phone?

Inside the dry bag, yes, but you will not be able to hold it on the slides. Rent an action camera or ask a guide to film you.

How do I pay?

A ₱500 deposit per person holds your seats, by bank transfer or e-wallet. The rest is paid in cash or by transfer on the morning of the trip.
