FAQ

Questions before you sign up

Still unsure? Send us a message and we'll answer the same day.

Do I need to buy new jugs?

No. Use your own round or slim 5-gallon jugs. We wash them and fit a new cap and seal for free when you sign up.

When is my free refill?

It's the first delivery on your plan. Your monthly bill starts from the second delivery.

Can I skip a week if I'm away?

Yes. Message us by the evening before your delivery day and we'll skip it — no charge for that week.

What if I run out before my day?

Message us for an extra drop-off. Inside the zones it's the normal refill price, no delivery fee.

How do I pay?

Monthly, in cash to your rider or by e-wallet / bank transfer. We send a simple receipt each month.
