Our process

Rice soaked overnight, ground at dawn.

No pre-mix powders. Every batch starts from whole glutinous rice and coconuts grated the same morning.

01

Soak overnight

Malagkit rice rests in water for twelve hours until it’s soft enough to grind by stone.

02

Grind to galapong

The soaked rice becomes a silky batter, mixed with fresh coconut milk and muscovado.

03

Steam in layers

Each colour layer is steamed on its own before the next is poured, so the slices stay clean.

Questions

Good to know

How long do kakanin stay fresh?

Enjoy them on the day of pick-up. Keep covered at room temperature; refrigerate biko and sapin-sapin overnight and warm briefly before serving.

Can I mix kinds in one bilao?

Yes. Small trays take 3 kinds, medium trays 4, and party trays come with all 6 arranged in wedges.

Do you deliver?

We don’t run our own riders, but we’ll pack your tray for the rider you book and hand it over at the counter.

Is bibingka available every day?

Bibingka is baked on Saturdays and Sundays. For weekday orders of 10 or more, send a pre-order two days ahead.
