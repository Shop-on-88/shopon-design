How it's made

Eight weeks, four benches

You get a photo at the end of each stage. The down payment is 50% after you approve the drawing; the rest is paid on delivery.

Week 1

Measure and draw

Send the room size and a photo. We return a scaled drawing with the seat height and arm width marked.

Pencil drawing, 1:10
Weeks 2–3

Steam and bend

Poles soften in the steam box for about 40 minutes, then dry for a week clamped on a wooden jig.

Steam box, bending jig
Weeks 4–6

Cane and bind

Seats and backs are caned strand by strand through drilled holes. Joints are wrapped with split peel.

Seven passes per seat
Weeks 7–8

Finish and deliver

Two coats of finish, a final check of every joint, and our own truck to your door in Luzon.

Delivery ₱1,500–₱4,500

Questions

Before you order

Do you deliver outside Luzon?

Yes, through a cargo forwarder to Visayas and Mindanao. We crate the piece; the forwarder's fee is quoted separately and usually takes 7–10 days.

Can I bring my own cushion fabric?

Bring 2 metres per lounge chair and we will sew the cushion with the frame. Our standard cushion is unbleached cotton canvas.

How do I look after rattan?

Dust with a soft brush, wipe with a damp cloth once a month and keep it out of direct afternoon sun. Once a year a thin coat of furniture oil keeps the cane from drying.

Can you copy a chair from a photo?

We can make a frame in a similar shape from your sketch or measurements. We do not reproduce another maker's design or label.
