FAQ

Questions we hear on every visit

Still unsure?

Send us a photo of your roof and bill. We'll reply with what we can already tell.

Message the team
Will the panels work during a brownout?

A grid-tied system switches off during a brownout for safety. If you want power during outages, we can quote a hybrid inverter with battery backup.

What happens in the rainy season?

Panels still produce on cloudy days, just less. Your quote shows a simple month-by-month estimate so you know what to expect.

Is my roof strong enough?

That's exactly what the free visit checks. If a part of the roof needs repair first, we'll tell you before you spend anything.

Do you offer installment payments?

Yes, staggered payments are available on most packages. We explain the options together with your written quote.

How long does installation take?

Most homes take 1–3 days on the roof. Net-metering approval by the utility can take longer, and we follow it up for you.
