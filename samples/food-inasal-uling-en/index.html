Our Fire

Three steps.
No shortcuts.

Good inasal is patient food. Every piece goes through the same three steps before it reaches your table, from the marinade bucket the night before to the last brush of oil over the coals.

STEP 01

Marinate overnight

Lemongrass, calamansi, garlic, ginger and coconut vinegar for twelve hours, so the flavour reaches the bone.

STEP 02

Baste in atsuete oil

Annatto seeds steeped in warm oil with garlic. It is what gives inasal its sunset colour and gentle sweetness.

STEP 03

Grill over uling

Slow-grilled over hardwood charcoal and turned by hand. Expect about fifteen minutes. It is worth the wait.
