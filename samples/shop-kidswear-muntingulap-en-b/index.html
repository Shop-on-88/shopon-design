Our cotton

Made the slow way,
washed until soft.

We keep our range small so every piece gets the same care: one fabric family, simple shapes, and details made for busy little bodies and the grown-ups dressing them.

Garment-washed

Every piece is pre-washed after sewing, so it feels soft from the very first wear and shrinks less at home.

Flat seams

Seams are flat-locked and labels are printed on, so there's nothing scratchy at the neck.

Easy snaps

Bottom snaps on every bodysuit and romper for quicker diaper changes, day or night.

Room to grow

Fold-back cuffs and a roomy cut let most pieces stretch across two sizes.

Care notes

Keep them soft,
wash after wash.

  1. Cool wash, inside outGentle cycle with mild detergent keeps colours calm and seams neat.
  2. Skip the bleach and softenerGarment-washed cotton is already soft — softener only coats the fibres.
  3. Dry in the shadeLine-dry away from strong afternoon sun so persimmon stays persimmon.
  4. Pass it onOutgrown but still lovely? Send it back and get ₱150 off your next order.
