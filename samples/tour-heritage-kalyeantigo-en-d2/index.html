Good to know

Questions before you go

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and we'll reply within the day.

What should I wear?

Comfortable shoes with good grip — cobblestones can be uneven. A hat and water for day walks; we lend umbrellas on hot or rainy days.

Is the walk okay for kids and seniors?

Yes. The route is flat with frequent shaded stops. The Kalesa & Courtyards walk is the easiest option for anyone who prefers to ride part of the way.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain: we walk with umbrellas and spend longer indoors at Casa Narra. Heavy rain or storm warnings: we move you to another date at no cost.

How do I pay?

Pay on the day in cash or by bank transfer after we confirm your booking. No payment is taken on this page.

Can I cancel?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before your walk. Just reply to your confirmation message.
