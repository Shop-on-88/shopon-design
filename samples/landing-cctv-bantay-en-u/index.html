05 · FAQ

Questions before you book

Still unsure? Send us a message and a technician will reply.

Is the site survey really free?

Yes. The survey and the camera plan are free with no obligation to install. We only ask that someone 18 or older is home during the visit.

Which areas do you cover?

Metro Manila and nearby Rizal towns. For other areas, send your barangay and city in the form and we'll check the schedule.

Will it work if the internet is down?

Yes. The recorder keeps saving footage to its hard drive. You just won't get live view on your phone until the internet is back.

Can I use my own cameras?

We can add compatible cameras you already own. The technician will check them during the survey.

Until when is the 10% off valid?

For surveys booked by December 31, 2026, with installation done by January 31, 2027.
