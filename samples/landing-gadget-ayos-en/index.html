[ 04 ] FAQ

Before you hand over your device.

Will I lose my photos and files?

Screen, battery and port work usually leaves your data untouched. For board or storage jobs we tell you first, and you can back up before we start.

Do I have to pay if I don't push through?

No. The diagnosis is free. If you decide not to repair, you take your device home at no charge.

Which brands do you repair?

Most major phone, tablet and laptop brands. Tell us the model in the form and we'll confirm parts before your visit.

Can I wait while you fix it?

Yes, for most screen, battery and port jobs. There's a bench-side seat so you can watch the work.

Is there a warranty?

Repairs come with a 30-day service warranty on the replaced part. Physical or water damage after pick-up is not covered.
