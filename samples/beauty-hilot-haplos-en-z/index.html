What hilot means here

An old Filipino way of touch, kept unhurried.

Hilot is the massage many of us grew up with — a lola's warm palms, coconut oil, a banana leaf by the stove. At Haplos we keep that feeling and give it a calm room, clean linens and enough time so no one watches the clock.

Good to know

Questions before you come

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and a therapist will reply.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. We provide a sarong-style wrap, disposable underwear and a robe. Your clothes stay in a private locker.

Is the coconut oil scented?

Our house oil is plain virgin coconut oil. If you'd like, we can add a few drops of lemongrass or ylang-ylang — just tell us at the foot soak.

Can I ask for a female or male therapist?

Yes. Add your preference when you book or tell us at the front desk, and we'll match you when available.

Do you do home service?

Yes, within Metro Manila for sessions of 60 minutes or more. We bring the oil, linens and a portable bed. Add ₱300 per visit.

How do I cancel or move my booking?

Text us at least 3 hours before your time and we'll move it at no charge.
