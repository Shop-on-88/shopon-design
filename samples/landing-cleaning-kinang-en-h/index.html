    FAQ

    Good to know

    Do I need to be home during the clean?

    Only at the start and for the final walk-through. Many clients leave a key with the building admin in between.

    What's the difference from a regular clean?

    A deep clean adds grout and tile scrubbing, inside-the-fridge and microwave, ceiling fans, window tracks and behind movable furniture.

    Is water and electricity okay to use?

    Yes, we use your unit's water and power. Everything else, from cloths to the vacuum, we bring.

    Can I reschedule?

    Free rescheduling up to 24 hours before your slot. Just reply to our confirmation text.
