01The counter

A bookmark between
the sea and the table.

Shiori means bookmark. Our counter marks a single page of the season: whatever the boats bring in that week, aged, cut and seasoned by hand, then served one piece at a time across ten seats of pale hinoki.

一

Rice first

Short-grain rice seasoned with red vinegar, kept at body warmth in a wooden ohitsu all service.

二

Fish rested, not rushed

Some fish are cured in kelp, some aged for days. Each is served when it tastes most like itself.

三

One piece, one moment

Nigiri is brushed with soy and placed in front of you. Eat it within a breath, while the rice is still warm.

03Fish of the month · November
Madai
Red sea bream · Autumn season

Firm, sweet and clean. In cooler months madai builds a layer of fat under the skin, which is exactly what we want. This month it appears three ways in every dinner course.

Kobujime nigiri

Cured

Pressed between sheets of kelp overnight for a quiet umami.

Matsukawa aburi

Seared

Skin briefly torched, finished with sudachi and sea salt.

Ushiojiru

Broth

A clear soup made from the bones, served as the course turns.

08Questions

Before you
sit down.

Anything else, send us a message. A person, not a bot, will reply.

Is there a dress code?

Smart casual. We ask guests to skip strong perfume or cologne, since the counter is small and aroma is part of every piece.

Can you work around allergies or pregnancy?

Yes. Tell us when we confirm your booking. We can prepare a fully cooked or no-shellfish course with a day's notice.

Are children welcome?

Guests aged 10 and up are welcome at lunch and the 18:00 dinner seating. Each guest takes a full course.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free changes up to 24 hours before your seating. Later cancellations or no-shows are charged 50% of the course, as fish is bought for each guest.

How can I pay?

Cash, debit and credit cards, bank transfer and major e-wallets. Private bookings settle the deposit by bank transfer.

May I bring my own wine or sake?

One bottle per two guests, with a corkage fee of ₱800 per bottle. We are happy to chill or warm it for you.
