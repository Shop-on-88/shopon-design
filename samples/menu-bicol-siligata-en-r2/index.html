Sili’t Gata Bicolano Kitchen

A neighborhood kitchen cooking Bicol dishes in fresh coconut milk and siling labuyo. A soot-black clay palayok of Bicol Express sits on a rough wooden table with split coconuts and loose chilies, and every dish carries a drawn chili mark so first-time guests can see how hot it runs before they order.
