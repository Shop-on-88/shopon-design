How delivery works

You see the scale before you pay

Every sack leaves our stall sealed. The rider opens nothing until you're there to watch the needle.

Step 1

Order before 10AM

Use the form below or message us. Tell us the variety, size and your street.

Step 2

We weigh it at your door

Our platform scale comes with the sack. Under 25.0 kg, we top it up from the salop right there.

Step 3

Carried to your kitchen

Up the stairs and into your rice bin if you want. Pay cash or e-wallet after you've seen the weight.

Before you order

What if I'm not home?

Leave a name of someone who can watch the weighing. We don't leave sacks at the gate.

Can I split a sack into two varieties?

Yes, 12.5 kg each in two sealed bags, priced per kilo of each variety.

Do you take back rice I don't like?

If the first kilo you cook isn't what you expected, call within 3 days and we'll swap the rest.
