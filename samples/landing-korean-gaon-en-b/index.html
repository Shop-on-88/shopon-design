Every week builds on the last. Homework takes about 15 minutes a day, and each class ends with something you can use outside the classroom.

Try it right now

Learn 3 letters
in one minute

Tap a letter to see how it is written stroke by stroke, then put two of them together. This is exactly how your first lesson starts.

  • Hangul has only 24 basic letters
  • Letters stack into square syllable blocks
  • Many shapes follow your mouth position

giyeok sounds like “g” in go

Two strokes: across, then down. Its shape shows the back of your tongue touching the roof of your mouth.

Memory tip: it looks like a gun pointing left — gun starts with g.

Quick check: which block reads “na”?

Questions

Before you
sign up

Still unsure? Send us a message and we will help you pick a class.

I know zero Korean. Is that okay?

Yes. The January course is made for complete beginners. We start with the very first letter and go step by step.

What happens in the free trial lesson?

You learn about 6 letters, read your first syllables and write your name in Hangul. You also see the classroom and meet the teacher. There is no payment and no obligation.

How old do I need to be?

Group classes are for ages 16 and up. For younger learners, a 1:1 class with a parent present is a good fit.

Can I switch from online to studio?

Yes, if there is a free seat in the studio class at your level. Just tell us a week before.

How do I pay for the course?

After your trial lesson you can pay at the studio by cash, bank transfer or card. Online students get a payment link by email.
