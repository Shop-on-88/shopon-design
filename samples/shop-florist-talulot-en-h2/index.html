Keeping them

A week in the vase, if you do four things

The same card goes inside every bouquet.

  1. Cut on a slantTrim two fingers off the stems before the first drink.
  2. Change the waterEvery second day, and rinse the vase while you are at it.
  3. Strip low leavesNo leaves under the waterline, they cloud it fast.
  4. Keep off the sillOut of direct sun and away from the electric fan.
