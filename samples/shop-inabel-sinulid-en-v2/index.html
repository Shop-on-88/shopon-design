How a double blanket gets made

Nine working days from the cotton cone to the shelf. Most of that is counting before the first pick.

1,280warp ends, each drawn by hand through a heddle and a dent in the reed
  1. 1 day

    Winding the warp

    Cones go onto the warping board and are counted off in bundles of forty, indigo and white in turn.

  2. 2 days

    Threading

    Two people at the loom: one passes each end, one hooks it through heddle and reed in pattern order.

  3. 5 days

    Weaving

    About 40 cm a day on the pedals, beating every pick with the reed so the binakol stays square.

  4. 1 day

    Finishing

    Fringe knotted by hand, a cold wash to settle the indigo, then a morning drying in the shade.

Single blankets take about seven days, runners about three.Shelf pieces ship the next working day.

Washing and keeping inabel

Cotton this dense gets softer with use. A few habits keep the indigo deep.

Cold water, mild soap

Hand wash or a gentle cycle. Wash indigo alone for the first two washes.

Dry in the shade

Hang over a line, out of hard noon sun, so the colours don’t fade on one side.

Iron on the reverse

Medium heat, while still slightly damp. Never iron the fringe.

Fold, don’t hang

Stored folded in its cotton bag. Refold along a new line each season.

Questions we get at the gate

Do you ship outside Ilocos?

Yes, anywhere in the Philippines by courier. Metro Manila usually arrives in three to five days; shipping is quoted when we confirm your order. For gifts abroad we pack flat for balikbayan boxes.

Is the dye going to run?

Indigo releases a little colour in the first two washes, then settles. Madder and charcoal are steadier. Washing cold and alone for the first rounds is enough.

Can I order a size you don’t list?

Widths are set by the loom, up to 75 cm per panel. Double blankets are two panels joined by hand. Lengths are flexible; tell us the bed or table and we suggest a size.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer or e-wallet for orders, cash at the loom house. Custom pieces take half up front and half before shipping.

Do you take orders for weddings or events?

Yes, for runners and wrapping cloths in matching colours. Give us at least two months for more than ten pieces.
