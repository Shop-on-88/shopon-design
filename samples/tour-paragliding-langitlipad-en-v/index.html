Your flight, minute by minute

Ninety minutes on the ridge, twenty of them in the sky.

No experience needed. Your pilot steers, you look around. If you want, they’ll hand you the brakes for a gentle turn over the lake.

20 MIN
01

Check-in & briefing

Weigh-in, waiver, and a five-minute talk on how to run and how to sit back once you’re up.

10 MIN
02

Gear up

Helmet, tandem harness and radio check. Your pilot clips you in and runs through it twice.

10–25 MIN
03

Run & fly

Three to five strides and the wing lifts you. Soar the ridge, then glide out over the water.

15 MIN
04

Land & ride back

Feet-first onto the grass field. Our jeep brings you back up while your clips download.

Gear & how we fly

Boring checks, so the flight can be the exciting part.

Every wing, harness and reserve is logged, inspected on a schedule, and packed by the pilot who flies it.

HELMETSPREADER BARTANDEM HARNESSRESERVERADIOWIND SOCK

Tandem wing & spreader

Wide, stable tandem canopy sized for two, with a spreader bar keeping you side by side.

Reserve parachute

A hand-deployed reserve rides on every tandem harness, repacked on a fixed schedule.

Helmet & radio

Hard-shell helmet for you, and the ground crew talks your pilot in for every landing.

Wind sock & station

Launch and landing both have a wind sock and a station we read before every lift-off.

The pilot can always say “not today.”If gusts pick up or the clouds drop, we wait or move your slot. You never pay for a flight that didn’t happen.

Questions people ask

I’ve never done anything like this. Is that okay?

You don’t need any experience. You only need to run a few steps on launch and lift your legs on landing. The pilot does the rest.

What if it’s too windy or rainy on my date?

We message you the evening before and again at 6 AM. If we can’t fly, you choose a new slot or get a full refund.

Are there age or weight limits?

Passengers from 12 years old with a guardian’s signature, and from 35 to 110 kg. Tell us about any injuries when you book so your pilot can plan.

Can my friends watch?

Yes. Spectators ride up for free and can watch from the launch deck or wait at the landing field café.

How far is it from Manila?

About two hours by car, depending on traffic. Book the van pickup and we’ll collect you at a meeting point.
